# Wayfinder 1.6: combat level is a routing constraint

## Incident and root cause

The supplied screenshot shows a level-22 character killed with a level-29 Fetid Corpse targeted. Earlier routing text explicitly selected Fetid Corpse as an item-drop source for a level-25 quest. The previous implementation filtered certain elite ranks, but did not check NPC min/max level when choosing the nearest drop source. Accepting above-range quests was therefore incorrectly treated as permission to route their combat targets. Quest level is not a reliable upper bound on the level of each possible item source.

The fix uses the current Forever provider's actual NPC fields, not a separate Classic-only list:

- [QuestieDB NPC metadata](https://github.com/Questie/QuestieDB/blob/master/src/meta/npcMeta.lua): `minLevel`, `maxLevel`, `rank`.
- [QuestieDB API contract](https://github.com/Questie/QuestieDB/blob/master/docs/api.md): numeric unknown/default semantics and fresh returned data.

No hand-authored exception for a single mob or quest substitutes for the general filter.

## Destination policy

The automatic combat cap is player level +2, independent of the new-quest comfort setting. At player level 22, a known maximum enemy level above 24 is excluded. The maximum of a provider range is used conservatively. Elite/rare-elite sources remain excluded in solo mode; group mode can permit appropriate elites but cannot relax the level cap. Boss sources are excluded. Missing or invalid combat level/rank data is unknown, not safe.

Drop-source alternatives are filtered first, then the closest appropriate source is selected. Thus a slightly farther suitable ghoul wins over a nearer level-29 source. Kill-credit alternatives follow the same rule. If an appropriate alternative exists, the objective is not falsely labeled held merely because another source was rejected.

The same checks cover direct creature objectives, NPC item drops, kill-credit targets and creature-referenced extra objectives. Generic/client coordinates cannot reintroduce a rejected combat target. Known combat rows with unverified event/waypoint source levels are held. High-level friendly vendors, object interactions and completed noncombat hand-ins are not treated as kill targets. This does not certify the safety of their surroundings.

A held objective remains in the character's quest log and diagnostic/deferred data. Held entries can appear in the bounded planned preview, with a resume level where calculable; clicking them cannot bypass the combat cap. A known level-29 source can be reconsidered at player level 27. If other objectives or sources are suitable, they remain usable. The normal region commitment is retained only among actionable steps.

## Live evidence and immediate interlock

The addon observes readable hostile Creature/Vehicle units through target, mouseover and nameplate tokens. A higher live level raises the effective provider bound. A lower later observation cannot erase a higher known bound. Skull observations are blocked without inventing an exact level. Classification merging preserves elite/boss severity rather than treating numeric rank order as danger order.

Observations are scoped to character, NPC/map and provider/build, expire after ten minutes and are bounded to 200 records. A single live level for an NPC with no provider maximum does not certify its full spawn range; such sources remain unverified for automatic combat routing. Positions represent the player's observation area, not an exact NPC location. Malformed saved evidence is ignored.

A currently visible readable hostile above the level cap, a skull enemy or a disallowed elite/boss creates a temporary navigation stop. Target/nameplate changes are handled immediately, and tracked units are polled approximately once per second. The danger decision has no waypoint; the owned map marker is released and the directional arrow is suppressed. Navigation resumes after the live threat is gone, while the source-level correction remains in force. An explicitly invisible distant selected enemy cannot leave the interlock stuck.

Safety revisions and source signatures invalidate forecast caches and point continuity. A previously committed or pinned source cannot survive once newly captured source data rejects it. Planner recovery still calls the freshly checked objective builder and cannot restore a rejected target. Automatic grind proposals additionally require recent appropriate enemy-level observations; a high kill rate alone is insufficient, including while an old grind commitment exists.

## Display and limits

Combat steps show the selected enemy's maximum level alongside the objective. The deferred status/tooltip explains held work; missing combat information is visible rather than converted into a navigation guess.

This release does **not** promise death-free leveling or safe traversal. The arrow is direct direction to an assessed destination, not terrain pathfinding, aggro-radius simulation or an escape route. It cannot discover every enemy along the road or behind terrain, predict scripted ambushes, or act on units the client does not expose. Threat warnings depend on readable target/mouseover/nameplate data. The player must still avoid enemies and retreat manually. No combat or movement is automated.
