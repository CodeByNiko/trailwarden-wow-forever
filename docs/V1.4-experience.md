# Wayfinder 1.4: measured XP and XP-aware questing

## Goals and data sources

Compare questing from current progress with a short local grind, and show actual XP/hour plus next-level ETA. Both accepted quests and eligible unaccepted quests participate. The planner reads the installed Forever QuestieDB catalog and its current corrections; no separate Classic-only quest/reward database is bundled. In XP mode it also considers eligible pickups in adjacent catalog zones while local actions exist. It does not enumerate arbitrary transport routes around the entire world.

Primary contracts reviewed:

- [Forever Unit API](https://github.com/Gethe/wow-ui-source/blob/forever/Interface/AddOns/Blizzard_APIDocumentationGenerated/UnitDocumentation.lua): UnitXP, UnitXPMax, PLAYER_XP_UPDATE and PLAYER_LEVEL_UP.
- [Forever quest-log events](https://github.com/Gethe/wow-ui-source/blob/forever/Interface/AddOns/Blizzard_APIDocumentationGenerated/QuestLogDocumentation.lua): QUEST_TURNED_IN supplies quest ID and reward XP.
- [QuestieDB support API](https://github.com/Questie/QuestieDB/blob/master/docs/api.md): Support.Get("QuestXP").db.
- [Current Questie XP consumer](https://github.com/Questie/Questie/blob/master/Database/QuestXP/QuestieXP.lua): colon-called GetQuestLogRewardXP and adjusted rewards.
- [Forever reward corrections](https://github.com/Questie/QuestieDB/tree/master/support/Forever/QuestXP), including new-quest trace corrections.

These are dependencies, not copied quest content. New Forever quests with missing rewards or positions remain visible through normal availability/active-quest handling; their XP projection is unknown. No missing reward is silently treated as zero. Provider coverage cannot be guaranteed by this addon.

## Actual XP/hour and ETA

The XP bar is the only source of session XP totals. Quest events and rewarded-kill chat events are used for attribution, not added again to the bar total. Rollover accounting adds the previous level remainder and current XP; a settling interval handles level/XP event order, with correction for delayed bar changes. Missing intermediate caps across multiple unseen levels are rebased rather than invented.

The active clock includes travel, death/recovery and ordinary downtime. Explicit addon pause and AFK are excluded, as are their XP gains. Offline time is not part of a session. A new login/reload starts a session; per-character pace learning persists. `/wf xpreset` resets session statistics without deleting learned pace.

After 60 seconds, the rate is XP gained over active elapsed time, using up to a rolling 15-minute window. The tooltip also shows the whole-session rate. ETA is remaining XP divided by the measured rolling rate. Zero XP/hour has no finite ETA; the level cap and unavailable APIs have separate statuses. ETA is an estimate of continued recent behavior, not a prediction that the upcoming quests will exactly reproduce it.

## Forward quest cycle

For every candidate quest, capture its reward source, pickup if unaccepted, remaining mapped goals and receiver. Adjusted rewards come from the installed Questie XP module when available. In combat/instances, the consumer may omit temporary bonuses; that projection stays low-confidence. A running grind break is held stable during combat until the next safe comparison or its time limit. A fallback uses the current provider's base reward with its consumer's grey-quest reduction; bonuses are explicitly unknown and confidence remains low.

A cycle includes the approach to pickup, objective visits, work, and travel to collect the reward. Travel uses map distance divided by learned movement speed, with a 20% detour allowance. It is not terrain pathfinding. Objectives are ordered by nearest remaining location. Accepted quests with compatible work areas and receivers within 200 yards can share a projected cycle; their rewards are counted once. Shared mob sources use the maximum shared effort/kill XP rather than summing the same kills twice. This is an approximation of overlapping objectives, not a probabilistic drop simulator.

Observed progress updates learn time per objective unit. Rewarded-kill intervals and actual movement samples update pace. Previously seen objective quantities can be reused on the same character/provider version. Completely unseen quantities use clearly low-confidence seeds: eight kill units, ten collection units, 35 seconds per kill, and two expected kills per dropped item until better observations exist. Event/object/vendor durations have similarly explicit heuristic seeds. These are generic cost priors, not a Classic quest database. Partial/unmapped required goals prevent a confident projection.

The projected rate is `(remaining quest rewards + estimated future kill XP) / remaining cycle time`. Past time/XP is not added to that future numerator. Work with reliable observed timing, verified kill samples where relevant and adjusted rewards can reach medium confidence; unseen quantities and unknown bonuses remain low confidence.

Modeled cycles rank by normalized XP return, discounted to 60% for low confidence. Direct distance is not charged twice after being included in cycle time. Unknown cycles keep normal heuristic scoring, so new quests are not filtered out just for missing XP data. Local batch protection, eligibility, skip/level-deferral, death/pause and manual focus remain constraints. Switching hysteresis avoids chasing small changes. A projection is for the remaining shared cycle, not XP for each row in the preview; do not sum preview rates/rewards.

## Grind comparison

Rewarded-kill messages use the client's localized COMBATLOG_XPGAIN format strings, without an English-only parser. Their numeric XP must fit the XP-bar delta after known quest rewards. Unverified residual gains may inform rough pace but cannot establish an automatic grind recommendation.

A grind comparison needs at least eight verified rewarded kills spanning two minutes in the same observed area. Observations reset after large moves or long gaps, and become stale without recent kills. The displayed grind rate is verified kill XP divided by active time since the area's first kill, including ordinary local downtime. This conservative sample does not assume the same result for another creature type, region or group.

Automatic grind suggestions require a medium-confidence quest-cycle alternative and a measured grind rate more than 25% higher. Low-confidence quest rewards/work do not automatically lose to grinding. A manual focused step prevents the override. A suggestion remains in the observed area, rechecks on normal events/maintenance, lasts at most three minutes and then has a two-minute cooldown. **Questing now** dismisses it immediately. The quest alternative stays in the preview. The recommendation does not attack, loot, accept quests or play the character.

## Display and controls

Two compact lines show rolling XP/hour, next-level ETA, the current projected quest cycle and observed grind rate. Hover for session totals, source counts and confidence. Settings contain XP-aware routing; `/wf xp` toggles that ranking while retaining measurement. `/wf xpreset` resets the session. Existing independent panel/arrow scaling remains. Adding the two lines increases the normal panel height by 30 units.

## Validation and limitations

270 checks: 154 scenario/UI integration, 40 pure engine, 21 provider/client contract, 55 XP accounting/projection. Sixteen serialized frames replay through the pure engine. Tests cover both level-up orderings and late correction, no quest double-count, mixed kill/quest events, localized and unverified kill messages, AFK/pause, rolling expiry, character isolation, new Forever-style reward IDs, unaccepted full-cycle costs, missing data, pace changes, shared rewards, XP ranking, grind margins, expiry/cooldown and live UI event wiring.

All fixtures are synthetic. No real Forever session trace is claimed. Actual provider reward coverage, server modifiers, drop rates, objective scripts and terrain still need gameplay validation. Future quest chains beyond current eligibility, future unlocks, transport timetables and all possible routes are not solved globally. Broader profiling/budgets (audit point 7) remain deferred.
