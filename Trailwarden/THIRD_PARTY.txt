CatalogIndex.lua contains a generated factual quest/entity/item/zone ID index
from the public API of QuestieDB Forever 1.0.14. No QuestieDB implementation,
quest names, coordinates or provider source files are bundled.
https://github.com/Questie/QuestieDB/releases/tag/v1.0.14
Source digest and reproduction: tools/generate_catalog_index.py and
docs/questiedb-implementation-1.8.4.md in the source repository.
